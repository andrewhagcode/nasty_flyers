Who we are

The improv is
the whole point

The Nasty Flyers are a Phish tribute band out of Rochester, New York, here to chase the jam wherever it wants to go.

Every show takes its own shape, builds its own peaks, and ends up somewhere nobody planned — band and crowd figuring it out together in real time.

The lineup brings real range. Miles McHugh (guitar, vocals) and Lou Chitty (keys, vocals) come out of the Dead tribute Into the Now. Tommy Mintel (drums, vocals) holds it down from Rochester neo-soul/funk outfit Helium Bubble. Chris Meeker (bass, vocals) has logged time in the Genesis tribute The Waiting Room and the Yes tribute Going for the One, and now plays with Lou and Miles in the original band Moving Mountains.

Between the prog, the funk, and the Dead in their bones, they've got the chops the catalog demands — and the instincts to take it off the rails when the moment's right.
